# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Indonesian UMKM owners, creators, and SMM agencies who need one place to plan, schedule, review, and understand social content. They arrive on a phone or laptop during busy workdays and need a clear next action without inflated promises.

## Product Purpose

Kelola Konten brings calendar planning, multi-platform publishing, inbox, analytics, and agency-ready reporting into one Indonesian-first workspace. The public marketing surface earns a waitlist signup and explains platform availability honestly while the product matures.

## Brand Personality

Clear, capable, approachable. The surface should feel calm enough for daily planning and concrete enough for an agency handoff.

## Anti-references

Avoid the archived green Buku Rapor direction on active marketing pages, generic SaaS card grids, copied Buffer visuals or wording, overused pills, numbered section markers, tiny UI illustration labels, em dashes, and unsupported claims about prices, approval, traffic, or active internal use.

## Design Principles

- Show the workflow in one glance: platform tiles, calendar language, and a single waitlist action.
- Be specific about what each platform can do and label audit-dependent features.
- Keep Indonesian copy warm and direct, with a useful next step in every section.
- Preserve an airy canvas and committed blue identity across routes.
- Treat agency reporting as a concrete output, not a vague promise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard-visible focus, readable contrast, labelled forms, semantic landmarks, reduced motion, mobile touch targets, and no horizontal overflow at 390px. Do not rely on color alone for status or platform availability.
