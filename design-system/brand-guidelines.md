# Kelola Konten brand guidelines

## Mark and wordmark

The active mark is a new Kelola Konten “K” tile created for the product. It is a compact blue rounded square with a white K formed by a stem and two angled arms. The geometry is intentionally original and is not copied from Buffer or the concept page. Use `brand-assets/kelola-mark.svg` as the source artwork. Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the mark width. Minimum digital size is 20px; preferred navigation size is 30px desktop and 24px mobile.

Use the wordmark in Figtree 700, 22px desktop and 15.5px mobile. Do not redraw the mark with text, add drop shadows, or stretch it. On dark footer surfaces use the provided dark-surface variant only.

## Color use

Brand blue `#1E5BD8` is the identity anchor and works for the mark, links, focus ring, icon tiles, and dark-surface action accents. Blue soft `#B3CCFF` is the primary CTA fill on the near-white canvas. Ink `#172338` is the reading color. Keep blue-soft text dark enough for AA contrast. Use official platform colors only inside labelled platform tiles.

The green Buku Rapor palette is archived. It is not an alternate active theme. Pink, yellow, gradients, and unlabelled status colors are disallowed.

## Type and voice

Figtree carries practical UI and body copy. Be Vietnam Pro carries the large display line. Indonesian copy is direct and specific. Use “Daftar” or “Gabung daftar tunggu” as the pre-launch CTA. State platform audit limits and planned pricing plainly. Do not use em dashes. Avoid hype, invented customer counts, and unsupported performance claims.

## Composition

The signature composition is a centered hero with a faint 54px grid and a small set of floating labelled utility tiles. The hero presents the work in one sentence, names the four platforms, then offers one waitlist field. Below it, two-column feature surfaces, a platform strip, a dark reporting panel, a support panel, resources, an about section, a blue-soft CTA, and a dark footer create a paced long-form marketing page.

Pills are reserved for the waitlist/search form and primary actions. Content cards, utility controls, and panels use 10–20px radii. Avoid side stripe decoration, nested cards, and repeated numbered section labels.

## Photography and illustrations

Use original screenshots, illustrations, or licensed imagery. Do not reuse concept page assets, Buffer assets, or text baked into reference screenshots. If a UI illustration contains copy, keep its smallest text at 12px and inspect at 390px and 1440px. Export raster UI artwork at 2x for DPR3 surfaces or use responsive SVG/HTML.

## Accessibility and QA

Maintain AA contrast, visible 3px focus rings, labelled fields, keyboard menu navigation, `aria-live` waitlist feedback, alt text, reduced-motion behavior, and no horizontal overflow at 1440px and 390px. Verify at least these states before release: invalid email, pending submission, success response, server error, open mobile menu, platform/API “Segera” label, and 404 route.
